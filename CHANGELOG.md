- Redesigned Shopping Lists, active checklists and Other functions with distinct layouts and transitions.

- Added a release archive to the account menu and grouped Personalization and My alerts under Settings.

- Added a bilingual animated update notice shown once per version and account in the current browser.

## 1.5.1

- Gave each Home widget a distinct layout, with calendar dates, document progress and day/night weather visuals.
- Added live widget reordering by touch or mouse, with other cards moving into place during the gesture.

## 1.5.0

- Redesigned Home as a customizable widget dashboard with add, hide, remove and reorder controls.
- Added weather via GPS or city search, personal reminders and quick links to trips and the NZ project.

## 1.4.0

- Separated paid and planned NZ project costs in the totals and entry lists.

- Added Today to travel mode with the day’s itinerary, next commitment, local departure details and voucher access.
- Made the offline PDF guide available from Today.

## 1.2.1
